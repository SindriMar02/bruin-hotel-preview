Our story

Brúin opened in 2013 and reopened in 2025

  1. 2013

    Brúin opens

    The restaurant opens on Hafnargata in June, close to the pier.

  2. 2022

    The hotel is added

    Hótel Grindavík opens in February. It has double rooms, family rooms with a sleeping loft and self check-in.

  3. 2023

    The town is evacuated

    Grindavík is evacuated in November, and the building stands empty.

  4. 2025

    Brúin reopens

    Brúin reopens in June. Coffee and soup are back on the menu, with tables beside the harbour-facing windows.

Restaurant and hotel

Restaurant Brúin

The restaurant seats 135 and serves fish dishes, meat dishes and burgers. It also has the Captain's fish soup, a children's menu, a play corner, coffee and home baking. The bar is always open.

Menu and bookings

Hótel Grindavík

Hótel Grindavík has double rooms and family rooms with a sleeping loft. Each room has a private bathroom, fridge and free Wi-Fi. Self check-in and free parking are available at the building.

Rooms and prices

Miðaftanhóll

The hill beneath the restaurant

Miðaftanhóll, the Hill of Midevening, lies beneath the restaurant. A hidden couple lives there and looks after the surrounding area.

They own a boat and fish from the harbour.
