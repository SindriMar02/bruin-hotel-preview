Hafnargata 26, Grindavík

One family, two houses

A restaurant and rooms at the front of the harbour, run by the same family in the same house.

Our story

Since 2013, with one interruption

  1. 2013

    Brúin opens

    The restaurant opens on Hafnargata in June, a stone's throw from the pier. Fish off the boats in the harbour from the first day.

  2. 2022

    The hotel is added

    Rooms open in February: double rooms and family rooms with a sleeping loft, with self check-in.

  3. 2023

    The town is evacuated

    Grindavík is evacuated in November and the house stands empty. Opening again was never a given.

  4. 2025

    The lights go back on

    The house opens again in June. Coffee on the pot, soup on the menu, and a table by the window facing the harbour.

The two houses

Restaurant Brúin

The room seats 135. Fish dishes and the Captain's fish soup, meat dishes and burgers, a children's menu and a play corner. A coffeehouse with home baking, and the bar is always open.

Menu and tables

Hótel Grindavík

Double rooms and family rooms with a sleeping loft, all with a private bathroom, fridge and free wifi. Self check-in and free parking at the house.

Miðaftanhóll

The house stands on a hill

Beneath the restaurant is a hill named Miðaftanhóll, the Hill of Midevening. A hidden couple live in it and look after the area around the hill.

They own a boat and fish from the harbour.
