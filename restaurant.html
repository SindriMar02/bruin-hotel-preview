Reopened in June 2025

Brúin opened at Hafnargata in June 2013. Grindavík was evacuated in November 2023, and Brúin reopened in June 2025.

We are open again. We serve coffee and home baking, with tables beside the harbour-facing windows.

Miðaftanhóll

Our neighbours in the hill

Miðaftanhóll, the Hill of Midevening, lies beneath the restaurant. The hill is home to a hidden couple and other elves. The couple look after the surrounding area and help other creatures when needed.

They own a boat and fish from the harbour.

To see the elves, you need three things: some joy in your heart, permission to let your inner child go out and play, and an elf who wants to be seen.

From the card that hangs in the dining room.

The house

The restaurant is a low, red-clad building at the front of the harbour. It has a terrace, a glass corner facing the sea, 135 seats, a children's play corner and a menu for groups.

Brúin opened at Hafnargata 26 in June 2013, close to the pier.

Find us

The restaurant is at Hafnargata 26, 240 Grindavík, at the front of the harbour and near the Blue Lagoon.

Opening hours can change. Call 426 7080 to be sure.
